Implemented rules and explicit assumptions

Time zone: America/New_York via the browser's IANA timezone support; not a fixed UTC offset.

Daily: 18:00–18:00 NY, split into four 6-hour quarters.

90-minute: each 6-hour Daily quarter contains four 90-minute cycles; each 90-minute cycle has four 22.5-minute quarters.

Micro: 22.5-minute cycle, split into four ~5.625-minute Nano cycles. Nano itself has four ~1.40625-minute quarters.

Weekly: cycle starts Monday 18:00 NY and spans four 24-hour quarters through Friday 18:00, matching the guide's Mon–Thu quarter naming.

Monthly: the guide anchors its True Open to the second weekly candle, counting from the first full weekly candle and ignoring partial first weeks; a fifth Monday is a joker week. For quarter labels, this prototype uses four week-buckets from the first Monday 18:00 in the month, clamped to Q4 for a fifth week. This needs source-map verification.

Yearly: the True Year Open is the first Monday in April at 18:00 NY. The prototype uses that anchor for a four-quarter year cycle.

Quarterly: special case. The guide says quarters are week-based, about three weeks each, with the final quarter 3–5 weeks and running alongside months. This prototype estimates quarters using the first full week boundary in each calendar quarter; this is the largest unresolved calendar approximation.

Quadrennial: the four-year calendar cycle anchor is not explicitly specified in the public excerpt. This prototype uses Jan 1 of years divisible by four, at 18:00 NY, as a provisional epoch. This must be validated against the official map.

Chains: a chain is at least three consecutive levels on the nine-level ladder sharing the same quarter, or adjacent levels in the allowed Q4/Q1 bridge. A skipped level breaks adjacency. Chain detection is independent from directional bias, SSMT, True Opens and price data.

Not included: live market prices, True Open price levels, NQ/ES/YM SSMT detection, PSP/qCISD triggers, alerts, or trading signals. Those require price data and separate rules.

Educational tooling only; not financial advice. Calendar assumptions marked above are provisional until verified against the official Daily Chain Map.
